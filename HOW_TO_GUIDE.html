Estimator App & Google Sheet — Team Guide
How to use the system from start to finish
Printed:

1. System Overview

The Toomey Estimator is an iPad app used in the field to build proposals and sync them automatically to Google Sheets. Every estimate submitted from the app becomes a permanent record in the sheet. The sheet is the single source of truth for all jobs.

ToolWhat It's ForWho Uses It
Estimator AppBuild proposals in the field, generate PDFs, sync to sheetEstimators
Google SheetTrack all estimates and service calls; office manages queueEveryone
FieldCentralDispatch, scheduling, and billing for all job typesOffice Manager + Estimators

Important: The app and Google Sheet stay in sync automatically. When an estimator submits a proposal, it appears in the sheet within seconds. The sheet should never need to be edited manually for estimates — only for service calls and the estimates queue.

2. Google Sheet — Tab Reference

The Google Sheet has five tabs. Each tab has a specific purpose. Do not move data between tabs manually — the app handles routing automatically.

TabWhat Goes HereWho Adds Entries
Estimates Queue Upcoming customer visits that need an estimate. Once an estimator completes the estimate in the app, the row is automatically removed from this tab and routed to the correct job tab. Office Manager
New Installs All completed new installation proposals. Auto-populated when estimator submits from app. App (automatic)
Re-Vamps All completed re-vamp proposals. Auto-populated when estimator submits from app. App (automatic)
Service Calls All service calls regardless of how they came in — app, phone, or email. This is the source of truth for all service call activity. Office logs phone/email calls here manually. App (automatic) + Office Manager (manual for phone/email)
System Recommendations All system recommendation proposals. Auto-populated when estimator submits from app. App (automatic)

Service Call Rule: Every service call — whether it comes in by phone, email, or through the app — must be logged in the Service Calls tab. This tab is the company's permanent record of all service activity. FieldCentral is where they get dispatched and billed, but the sheet is where they are tracked.

3. Office Manager Workflow

Office

A. Managing the Estimates Queue

The Estimates Queue tab is how the office communicates upcoming visits to the estimators. When a customer calls to request an estimate, the office adds a row to the queue. The estimator sees it in the app before they arrive.

1
Open the Estimates Queue tab in Google Sheets.
2
Add a new row with: Customer Name, Address, Phone, Email (if available), Scheduled Date, Job Type, and any Notes for the estimator.
3
The row will appear in the estimator's app automatically within a few minutes. No other action needed.
4
When the estimator completes the visit and submits from the app, the row is automatically removed from the queue and moved to the correct job tab (New Installs, Re-Vamps, etc.).

Job Types for the Queue: New Install, Re-Vamp, Service Call, System Recommendations. Use the exact spelling so the app routes the job correctly.

B. Logging Service Calls (Phone or Email)

When a service call comes in that was not submitted through the app, the office logs it directly in the sheet.

1
Open the Service Calls tab in Google Sheets.
2
Add a new row with: Customer Name, Address, Phone, and a brief Issue Description.
3
Log the call in FieldCentral for dispatch and billing.

Remember: The Service Calls tab in Google Sheets is the master record. FieldCentral is where the call gets dispatched and billed. Both need to be updated — the sheet for tracking history, FieldCentral for operations.

C. FieldCentral — Dispatch & Billing

All jobs — new installs, re-vamps, service calls, and system recommendations — are dispatched and billed through FieldCentral. The Google Sheet and Estimator App are for tracking and proposal generation only. FieldCentral is the operational system.

  • Service calls come in → log in Sheet → create job in FieldCentral → dispatch tech
  • Estimate accepted → create job in FieldCentral → schedule install
  • All invoicing happens in FieldCentral

4. Estimator Workflow

Bill · Jerry · Colin

A. Getting Started — Sign In

The app is saved to your iPad home screen. Open it like any app.

1
On the home screen, tap your name.
2
Enter your email password when prompted. This is stored on your iPad only and is used to send proposals via email.
3
You only need to sign in once. The app remembers you until you sign out.

B. Starting an Estimate from the Queue

If the office has scheduled a visit and added it to the Estimates Queue, it will appear in the app before you arrive. This is the preferred workflow.

1
Tap Estimates Queue on the home screen.
2
Find your customer's card and tap Start Estimate. The app will pre-fill their name, address, and job type automatically.
3
If the job type is already known (e.g. New Install), the app skips straight to the estimate screen. No extra tapping needed.

C. Starting a Walk-Up Estimate

For customers not in the queue, start a new estimate manually.

1
Tap New Estimate on the home screen.
2
Select the job type: New Installation, Re-Vamp, Service Call, or System Recommendations.
3
Enter the customer's name, address, and email (email is optional but needed to send the proposal).

D. Filling Out the Estimate

New Installation

  • Enter head counts: PGP-Ultra Rotary, MP-Rotor, Pro-4 Spray — tap + / − or type the number directly.
  • Enter right-side values: Drip Zones, Number of Zones, Irritrol Valves, and select the controller (Hydrawise timer).
  • Enter the Price. Numbers only — no $ sign needed.
  • Add Notes if needed (these print on the proposal).
  • Add-ons: Sidewalk Strip and Other (optional).

Re-Vamp, Service Call, System Recommendations

  • Enter customer info, then select materials and labor from the item list.
  • Adjust quantities with + / −.
  • Add notes as needed.
  • A PDF is generated on your device (no internet required for this step).

E. Generating and Sending the Proposal

1
Tap Generate Proposal. For New Installs, this fills the Toomey template PDF. For other types, a PDF is built on-device.
2
Tap Preview to review the PDF before sending. Tap Download to save it to your iPad's Files app.
3
If the customer's email was entered, tap Send to Client. The proposal is emailed directly from your address.
4
Add a personal note before sending if you want to include a custom message in the email body.
5
A green "Synced to Google Sheets" notification confirms the estimate was recorded. If you see a red warning, the sync failed — check your connection and note the job details manually.

If you need to edit after generating: Tap Edit on the done screen to go back and make changes, then regenerate. The sheet record will be updated, not duplicated.

F. App Updates

When an update is available, a blue bar will appear at the top of the app saying "App update available — tap here to refresh." Tap it once and the app reloads with the latest version. You do not need to delete and re-add the app from your home screen.

6. Common Issues

ProblemWhat to Do
Estimates Queue is empty or not loading Tap the ↻ Refresh button in the top-right of the queue screen. The app shows when the queue was last updated. If still empty, check your internet connection.
Red "Sheet sync failed" notification The estimate was saved to your iPad but did not reach Google Sheets. Note the customer name and details. Re-submit when you have a connection, or notify the office to log it manually.
App seems outdated compared to another device Wait for the blue update banner at the top of the app and tap it. If no banner appears, close Safari fully and reopen the app.
PDF preview won't open On the iPad, go to Settings → Safari → Block Pop-ups and turn it off. The preview opens in a new tab.
Email send fails Sign out and sign back in to re-enter your email password. If it continues to fail, download the PDF and email it manually from your email app.
