How the Whole System Works

Every estimate starts with a customer call and ends with a proposal in their inbox — all tracked automatically in one Google Sheet. Here is the full path from start to finish:

📞
Customer Calls
Office takes the request
📋
Office Queues Visit
Added to Google Sheet with notes & job type
🚐
Tech Dispatched
Opens app, sees visit with office notes
📱
Estimate Built
Photos, materials, labor added on iPad
📄
Proposal Sent
PDF emailed to customer from app
✅
Customer Accepts
Clicks link — sheet updates automatically
📅
Job Scheduled
Office books the work
The Google Sheet is the hub for everything. The app automatically sends every submitted proposal to the correct tab in the sheet — no manual copying. Office staff work entirely in the sheet. Field techs work entirely in the app. They stay in sync automatically.

The Four Job Types

Every estimate fits into one of four categories. Each one has its own tab in the Google Sheet and its own proposal format.

🌱 New Installation
🔧 Re-Vamp
🛠 Service Call
💡 System Recommendations
Job TypeWhen to use itWho typically does it
🌱 New Installation Customer wants a brand new irrigation system installed. Full design with head counts, zones, and drip. Estimator
🔧 Re-Vamp Larger-scope upgrade to an existing system. Requires equipment or machine work. Field Tech or Estimator
🛠 Service Call Repair or urgent work that needs to be done. May include materials, labor, and tax. Field Tech
💡 System Recommendations Upgrades or fixes the tech spots while on a job — things the customer didn't ask for but should know about. Field Tech
System Recommendations tip: These are great for uncovering revenue the customer doesn't know they need. If you see blocked heads, a WiFi controller upgrade opportunity, or worn solenoids — log it right there in the app. It takes 2 minutes and sends the customer a professional proposal automatically.

Office Staff

Scheduling visits, tracking proposals, and monitoring job status

Part 1 — Adding a Visit to the Estimates Queue

When a customer calls to schedule an estimate or service visit, you add a row to the Estimates Queue tab in the Google Sheet. The field tech will see it automatically in the app — no need to call or text them separately.

  • 1
    Open the Toomey Estimator Hub Google Sheet. You can find it in your Google Drive or bookmark it in your browser.
  • 2
    Click the Estimates Queue tab at the bottom of the sheet.
  • 3
    Click on the first empty row below the last entry. Fill in each column left to right:
ColumnWhat to enterRequired?
Customer NameFull name of the customerYes
AddressFull service addressYes
PhoneBest contact numberYes
EmailCustomer email — needed to send them the proposalYes
Scheduled DateDate of the visit (e.g. 6/12/2026)Yes
Job DescriptionBrief description of what the visit is for (e.g. "Check zone 3, possible head damage")Recommended
Assigned ToName of the tech going outRecommended
StatusLeave as Scheduled when adding. Will auto-update to Completed when tech submits proposal.Yes
NotesInternal notes for the tech. What did the customer tell you? What are they hoping for? What should the tech look for on site? These notes appear in the app but do NOT go on the proposal sent to the customer.Recommended
Job TypeSelect from the dropdown: New Install, Re-Vamp, Service Call, or System Recommendations. This tells the app which form to open automatically — the tech skips the selection step entirely.Strongly recommended
How the tech sees your notes
When the tech opens the Estimates Queue in the app, your notes show up in a highlighted yellow box on their screen labeled "Office Notes (Internal)." They read it before tapping Start Estimate. The notes are only for the tech — they never appear on the PDF sent to the customer.
Do not leave the Job Type column blank if you know what the visit is for. If it is blank, the tech will still need to manually pick the job type themselves in the app. Filling it in saves them a step and makes sure the estimate goes to the right sheet tab automatically.

Part 2 — Monitoring Proposals in the Sheet

Once a tech submits an estimate from the app, it automatically appears in the correct tab of the Google Sheet. You do not need to do anything — it's just there.

Part 3 — When a Customer Accepts a Proposal

  • 1
    The customer receives an email with the proposal PDF attached and a button that says Accept This Proposal.
  • 2
    When they click the Accept button, the Status column in the sheet automatically updates to "Accepted." You do not need to do anything — it happens on its own within seconds.
  • 3
    Check the sheet regularly and sort by Status to find all proposals marked Accepted that are waiting to be scheduled. Reach out to the customer to book the job.
For New Installations: The New Installs tab also has a Stage column with a dropdown. Once a customer accepts, the Stage updates automatically to "Accepted." You can then manually advance it through: Scheduled → Completed → Post-Install Follow Up as the job progresses. This feeds the Kanban board in the Dashboard.

Part 4 — Tracking Service Calls (Resolved / Unresolved)

The Service Calls tab has a special Resolved column (the last column). Every service call starts as Unresolved (highlighted red) automatically.

  • 1
    When a service call job is completed and closed out, find the row in the Service Calls tab.
  • 2
    Click on the cell in the Resolved column for that row. A dropdown will appear with two options: Unresolved and Resolved.
  • 3
    Select Resolved. The entire row will turn green automatically.
You can also add service calls manually. If a service call comes in and never goes through the app (for example, a walk-in or a verbal job that gets handled on the spot), you can type a row directly into the Service Calls tab. Just leave the Firebase ID, Accept Token, and Accept URL columns blank. Everything else works the same — it will show up in the Dashboard and leaderboard just like any other entry.

Part 5 — The Dashboard

The Dashboard gives you a live overview of the business. You can access it in two ways: tap Dashboard in the app, or open dashboard.html in a browser.

At the top, you can filter by date range. Use the quick buttons (Today, Last 7 Days, This Month, etc.) or enter a custom date range. Everything on the page updates to match the filter.

Field Technician

Estimates Queue, on-site proposals, service calls, and system recommendations

Part 1 — Setting Up the App (First Time Only)

  • 1
    On your iPad, open Safari and go to: colins4220.github.io/Toomey-estimates
  • 2
    To add it to your home screen so it works like a regular app: tap the Share button (the box with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom of Safari, then tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add. The Toomey icon will appear on your home screen.
  • 3
    The first time you open the app, it will ask you to select your name. Tap your name from the list.
  • 4
    It will ask for your email password. This is your Toomey email password (the same one you use to log into your email). The app uses it to send proposals from your email address. You only enter this once — the app remembers it.
  • 5
    You are in. From now on, just open the app from your home screen icon and it will know who you are automatically.
Need to switch users? Tap your first name in the top-right corner of the app. A confirmation will appear. Tap yes to switch to a different user. This is useful if more than one tech shares an iPad.

Part 2 — Checking Your Estimates Queue

The office schedules all upcoming visits in the Estimates Queue. This is your to-do list for the day. Always check it first.

  • 1
    On the home screen, tap Estimates Queue.
  • 2
    You will see a list of all scheduled visits. Each card shows the customer name, address, phone number, scheduled date, and job description.
  • 3
    If the office left notes, you will see a yellow box labeled "Office Notes (Internal)" on the card. These are internal notes from the office about what the customer is looking for or what to check on site. Read these before arriving. They do not appear on the proposal you send to the customer.
  • 4
    When you are ready to start an estimate for a visit, tap the Start [Job Type] → button on that card. If the office set a job type, the app will automatically open the right form with the customer's info already filled in. If no job type was set, you will be taken to the home screen to pick one.
Example of what you will see
"Customer says zone 4 has low pressure. Also noticed a broken head near the driveway last fall. Check backflow preventer as well. They are interested in upgrading the controller to WiFi if price is reasonable."

Part 3 — Building a Service Call, Re-Vamp, or System Recommendation Estimate

These three job types all use the same form in the app. Here is how to fill it out step by step.

  • 1
    Customer Info: If you started from the Estimates Queue, the customer name, address, phone, and email are already filled in. If not, you will be asked to enter them. Make sure the email is correct — this is where the proposal will be sent.
  • 2
    Photos: Tap Add Photo to attach photos of the site, problem area, or work completed. You can add multiple photos. These appear in the proposal PDF.
  • 3
    Materials: Type in the search box to find a part or material. Tap it to add it to the list. Use the + and − buttons to set the quantity. The price is calculated automatically. To remove an item, tap the ✕ next to it.
  • 4
    Labor: Enter the number of hours and confirm the hourly rate. The labor total is calculated automatically.
  • 5
    Notes: Enter any notes about the work — what you found, what you did, or what you recommend. These notes DO appear on the proposal the customer receives, so write them in a customer-friendly way.
  • 6
    Review the total at the bottom. When everything looks right, tap Submit Job. The estimate is saved to Firebase and synced to the Google Sheet automatically.
  • 7
    After submitting, tap Preview PDF to see the proposal before sending. If it looks right, enter the customer's email address (it may be pre-filled), add an optional personal message, and tap Send to Customer.
  • 8
    The proposal is emailed to the customer from your Toomey email address. It includes the PDF and an Accept This Proposal button. When the customer clicks it, the status in the Google Sheet updates automatically.
Always tap Submit before sending. The Submit button saves the job to the records first. The Send button only sends the email. If you only send without submitting, the job will not appear in the Google Sheet.

Part 4 — Looking Up a Past Proposal

If a customer calls and asks about a proposal from earlier in the season — or you need to reference something from a past visit — you can look it up right in the app.

  • 1
    On the home screen, tap Past Proposals.
  • 2
    Type the customer's name, address, or any other detail in the search box and tap Search.
  • 3
    A list of matching proposals will appear, showing the job type, date, and total.
  • 4
    Tap any result to open the full proposal detail — including all photos, materials, labor, notes, and totals, exactly as they were submitted.

Estimator — New Installations

Full irrigation system design and pricing

Overview

The New Installation flow is a dedicated multi-step process built specifically for designing and pricing a new irrigation system. It is separate from the other job types and has its own pricing logic built in.

Step-by-Step: Building a New Installation Estimate

Getting Started

  • 1
    Check the Estimates Queue first. If the office queued this visit and set the job type to "New Install," the app will open the New Installation form automatically when you tap Start Estimate. Customer info will be pre-filled.
  • 2
    If you are not starting from the queue, tap New Installation on the home screen.

Step 1 — Customer Information

  • 3
    Enter the customer's full name.
  • 4
    Enter the service address. As you type, address suggestions will appear below the box — tap the correct one to select it. This auto-fills the full address.
  • 5
    Enter the customer's email address. Double-check this — it is where the proposal PDF will be sent.
  • 6
    Tap Next →

Step 2 — System Design

  • 7
    Use the + and − buttons to enter the head counts for each type:
Head TypeWhat it is
Hunter PGP-Ultra RotaryStandard rotary head — most common for lawns
Rotor Streamer (MP Nozzle)Multi-stream rotary nozzle for tighter areas
Hunter Pro-4 SprayFixed spray head — used in beds and narrow strips
Drip ZonesNumber of drip irrigation zones
Number of ZonesTotal number of irrigation zones / valve stations
  • 8
    Pricing is calculated automatically based on the total head count:
    • 1–15 heads: $2,650 base price
    • 16–30 heads: $3,635 base price
    • 31+ heads: $125 per head
    • Each drip zone adds $1,000
    You can override the price manually if needed by tapping on the price field and typing a new number.
  • 9
    Tap Next →

Step 3 — Add-Ons

  • 10
    If there are any additional items to add (sidewalk strip work, specialty items, etc.), enter a description and a price. You can add as many as needed. If there are no add-ons, just tap Next → to skip.

Step 4 — Review and Generate

  • 11
    Review the full summary — customer info, all head counts, add-ons, and the final total. Make sure everything is correct.
  • 12
    Add any Notes if needed. These will appear on the proposal.
  • 13
    Tap Generate Proposal. The PDF is created and the proposal is saved to the New Installs tab in Google Sheets automatically. The Stage will be set to "Estimate Sent" in the sheet.
  • 14
    Tap Preview PDF to review it before sending.
  • 15
    When it looks right, tap Send to Customer. The proposal is emailed to the customer with the PDF attached and an Accept This Proposal button.
When the customer accepts: The Status in the sheet updates to "Accepted" and the Stage on the Kanban board moves to "Accepted" automatically. The office will see this and schedule the installation.

The Dashboard

The Dashboard gives you a live picture of how the business is performing. It reads directly from the Google Sheet so it is always up to date.

How to Access It

  • In the app: Tap Dashboard on the home screen.
  • In a browser: Open dashboard.html from the ToomeyEstimator folder.

Using the Date Filter

At the top of the dashboard you will see date filter buttons: Today, Last 7 Days, This Month, Last Month, This Year, All Time. Tap any of them and the entire dashboard updates immediately to show only data from that time period. You can also tap Custom and enter your own start and end dates.

The Kanban Board (New Installs Pipeline)

The bottom section of the dashboard shows all New Install leads organized into columns by stage. Each card shows the customer name, price, and date.

To move a card to a different stage:

  • 1
    Tap the card you want to move. It will highlight with a blue border to show it is selected.
  • 2
    Tap the column heading you want to move it to (for example, tap Scheduled to move it to the Scheduled column).
  • 3
    The card moves immediately and the Google Sheet updates in the background. No save button needed.

Troubleshooting

App Issues

ProblemWhat to do
App won't load / blank screen Close Safari completely (swipe it away in the app switcher) and reopen. If still not working, check your internet connection.
Estimates Queue is empty The office needs to add a row to the Estimates Queue tab in the Google Sheet. The app shows exactly what is in that tab.
Email send fails Your email password may be wrong. Tap your name in the top-right corner of the app, confirm switching users, then select your name again and re-enter your password carefully.
Proposal not appearing in the sheet The sync requires an internet connection at the moment you tap Submit. Make sure you have Wi-Fi or cellular data. Try submitting again — it will not create a duplicate if the job was already saved.
Customer info did not pre-fill from queue This can happen if the app loaded before the queue refreshed. Go back to the Estimates Queue screen and tap Start Estimate again.

Google Sheet Issues

ProblemWhat to do
Status did not update when customer accepted Wait 30 seconds and refresh the sheet. If it still has not updated, the customer may not have clicked the link yet — or their email went to spam.
Headers are missing or columns are out of order Contact the person who manages the App Script. They can re-run "setupHeaders" to restore all headers and dropdowns.
Dropdown is missing from Status or Job Type column Same as above — the "setupHeaders" script needs to be re-run.
Row color is not changing to green after marking Resolved Make sure you are selecting from the dropdown (click the cell, then click the dropdown arrow) rather than typing. The conditional formatting only triggers on exact values.
